II  ·  My judgmentsOn each of the five

The five core values are locked; nothing below changes them. What follows is my color-eye for the Founder and Claude to weigh — to be acted on, deferred, or ignored.

Ink Black #111111
Hold. The 6% lift off pure #000000 reads as ink-on-paper rather than screen-on-pixel. It pairs correctly with Paper. No adjustment recommended.
Paper White #F7F3EA
Hold. The warm cast (yellow + a touch of red, almost no blue) gives the surface a printed quality. On retina displays in a daylit room it reads as off-white book stock. The only risk is OLED phones at night, where it can read amber — but that’s correct for KEY’s tone.
Warm Gray #BDB6AA
Hold — but mind the role. At ~1.9:1 contrast on Paper, this is not body type. It’s a hairline / border / decorative color. The brief already treats it that way. If we later need a true reading secondary, use Ink 500 (#5A554F) from the scale below — not Warm Gray.
Deep Burgundy #6E1F2A
Hold. Worth a printer’s wet proof — the four-color build leans purple on coated stock. The Pantone match (1815 C) gives a slightly redder result and is the safer choice for any Founder Edition. Reads correctly on screen.
Night Navy #141923
Hold. Slightly cool relative to Paper’s warmth — intentional. The contrast is what reads as document, not theme. Keep it strictly for the surfaces the brief names (covers, admin); not the open web.

VI  ·  Usage rulesWhat goes where

Ink Black
Primary type at every weight. Wordmark in default lockup. Section dividers as 0.5 pt rules at large breaks.
  • Use for: body, headings, wordmark, callouts.
  • Do not use for: large color fields, button fills (KEY has no buttons that look like SaaS buttons), background of the open web.
Paper White
The site, the brief, the print page. Use Paper 200 (#F7F3EA) as canonical; Paper 100 (#FBF8F1) only as a slight lift for cards or quotes; Paper 300+ only for dividers and sunken regions.
  • Do not: use pure white anywhere. Pure white reads as “app.”
Warm Gray
0.5 pt hairlines (dividers, table borders, footer rules), and decorative captions/labels at 13 pt and up. Use Ink 500 — not Warm Gray — for any secondary type that has to be read.
Deep Burgundy
Always a point, never a plane.
  • Use for: stamps and seals, roman numerals, the left-rule of a blockquote, the hairline between sections of a Brief, the wax-stamp number on a cover, the redaction mark on the wordmark.
  • Do not use for: CTA backgrounds, hero gradients, full-bleed cover fields, any large area larger than a coin.
Night Navy
Reserved surfaces only.
  • Use for: KEY Brief PDF cover, KEY Letter cover, admin dashboard, investor deck dark pages.
  • Do not use for: any public-web page, any marketing surface, any email background.
Absolute prohibitions
  • Any color outside the five (and their derived scales).
  • Gradients of any kind — including 5%-to-5% “subtle” ones.
  • Overlay opacity below 95% (the system is opaque ink, not glass).
  • Blue-violet “AI” tints. #5B6EF5, #7B68EE, and their cousins are banned.
  • Glassmorphism, frosted backdrops, drop shadows on color.
  • Status colors (success-green, warning-orange, danger-red) introduced ad-hoc. None until a product flow demands it.
