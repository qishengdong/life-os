# KEY · Photographer Brief · Brief 04 Image (Direction A)

**For**: the photographer commissioned to produce the Founder-Edition still life.
**Image**: 1 frame. B/W, grain, dark tonal range. To be dropped into the KEY internal demo poster, replacing the current typographic placeholder.

---

## The brief in one line

A desk, just abandoned. Someone has been writing something heavy. They have walked away, briefly. We are reading the silence they left.

---

## Props

**On the desk** (all must be in frame, arranged with intention — not staged):

- **A fountain pen**, capped, resting horizontally on a folded piece of A4 paper or beside it. Acceptable: Montblanc Meisterstück 149, Pelikan M800, Sailor 1911 long. *Not acceptable*: Pilot G2, Uni-Ball, any ballpoint, any visibly modern click-pen.
- **A folded sheet of A4 paper**, off-white or cream (not pure white). One corner folded over, slight crease, no visible text.
- **A teacup, half full**. Acceptable: a simple white porcelain teacup with thin walls, or an unfussy Chinese 青花 (blue-and-white) cup. Tea visible, slight steam rising or none — both acceptable. *Not acceptable*: mug, takeaway cup, any logo.
- **A desk lamp**, only the base or the lower cone visible — the bulb itself out of frame. Acceptable: brass-finished or walnut-base reading lamp, classic gooseneck or banker's-lamp silhouette. *Not acceptable*: IKEA-modern, LED strip, anything visibly contemporary.

**Surface**: dark wood (walnut, teak, or stained oak). Visible grain is fine — encouraged. Not lacquered to a mirror finish.

**Not in frame** (zero tolerance):

- Phone, laptop, tablet, headphones, watch, glasses, any modern technology
- Any visible logo (pen brand markings should be obscured or out of focus)
- Hands, faces, any part of a person
- Plants, flowers, decorative objects
- Anything that suggests the image was shot in 2026 specifically — keep it timeless

---

## Lighting

- **Single warm light source**, positioned upper-right at ~45°
- High contrast — most of the frame is in shadow, with the pen, the paper's folded edge, and the teacup picking up the light
- No natural daylight (no window light, no "morning office" softness)
- No fill light from the opposite side; let shadows fall
- Lamp may or may not be the in-frame light source — your call

---

## Composition

- **Aspect ratio**: 3:2 horizontal, to be cropped down to ~1.96:1 for the poster
- **Desk surface**: lower 70% of the frame
- **Upper 30%**: dark space (the room beyond the lamp's reach), with the lamp's lower cone visible at top edge
- **Object placement**: asymmetric. Left third holds the pen + paper. Right two-thirds holds the lamp base + teacup, with negative space between them. The image breathes; objects are not crowded.
- **Eye line**: the viewer is the person who just left this desk. Camera at sitting height, slightly above the desk plane (≈ 105 cm), looking down-and-across.

---

## Tonal range

- **Black-and-white**, no colour
- **Grain**: visible. Reference: Kodak Tri-X 400, pushed +1 to ASA 800. If shooting digital, apply Tri-X-style grain in post (Silver Efex Pro preset "Tri-X pushed +1", or equivalent).
- **Shadows**: deep. Black point should clip very slightly — we want dense blacks, not "lifted shadow" digital look.
- **Highlights**: not blown. The pen's brass should retain texture; the teacup's white should retain form.
- **Mid-tones**: warm-leaning even in black-and-white — this is achieved in post by selecting a slight sepia split-tone (5–8% magenta in shadows, 2% yellow in highlights). Subtle. The image should read as silver halide, not as a digital filter.

---

## What the image should make the viewer feel

Not loneliness. Not melancholy. *Aftermath of careful thought.*

The reader of the poster should look at the photograph and feel that they have walked into a room where someone has just finished writing something important — and they themselves are the next to sit down.

---

## Deliverables

- **Master file**: 16-bit TIFF, 4961 × 3307 px (covers 300 DPI at A2 image area), pre-grain
- **Print version**: 8-bit TIFF, 4961 × 3307, with grain baked in
- **Digital version**: PNG, 1417 × 945 px, with grain baked in (for the poster's digital preview)
- **Working files**: layered PSD / Capture One session, for any post-shoot revision

---

## Reference frames (mood)

Looking at — but **not reproducing**:

- Karl Blossfeldt's *Urformen der Kunst* — for the close, considered framing
- Josef Sudek's Prague window series — for the sense of light retreating
- The Drift Magazine's "Inheritance" issue cover (Issue 4) — for the still-life-as-portrait approach
- Aperture's *Aging* issue 235 — for the tonal restraint

---

## What we are *not* going for

- Sepia "vintage" Instagram filter
- "Cozy reading nook" lifestyle photography
- Hipster studio-aesthetic (no plants, no marble, no exposed brass)
- "Productivity" desk-setup photography
- Soft-focus or shallow DOF as a substitute for composition

---

## Process

1. Pre-shoot conversation with the Founder — 30 minutes, confirm props sourced, confirm desk surface.
2. Shoot — half day, multiple compositions, single subject set.
3. Edit — the Founder + design select two finalists.
4. Final post — grain, tonal split, master TIFF + print TIFF + digital PNG.

---

## Budget bracket

Standard editorial commission for a single B/W still life by a working photographer. The Founder will negotiate directly. Aim for someone whose recent magazine work includes 财新, 三联生活周刊, or international equivalents at the level of Monocle or Aperture.
