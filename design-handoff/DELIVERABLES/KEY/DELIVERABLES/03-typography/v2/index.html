Typography V2

Plan A locked. Two precise lifts from V1: body lh 1.78 → 1.75, and a new body-large step at 19 px for KEY Letter long-form. Everything else unchanged.

I  ·  What changed since V1Two lifts, no drama

  1. Body line-height pushed back to 1.75. V1 raised it to 1.78 with the note “set to settle.” That wasn’t a real reason. 1.75 is what the Brand Brief and Master Directive both specified, and at 17 px with Source Serif 4 it’s the publication standard. Accepted.
  2. Added body-large step — 19 px / 1.80 EN, 19 px / 1.92 CN. For KEY Letter (Brief 15), /methodology long-form, and any surface where 17 px feels tight. Sustained-reading register.

Plan A is now annotated as adopted in the recommendations file. No font changes. No new families. No new weights. The full variable-axes import URL (200–900, 8..60 opsz, both italic and roman) stays as V1 shipped it.

II  ·  Body lh · before / after1.78 → 1.75

The same paragraph, two leadings. The shift is small. Over a Brief’s nine sections it matters.

V1  ·  lh 1.78

You came in asking whether to leave the job. The job is the symptom. The decision underneath — and the only one you can act on this quarter — is whether to lower the cost of being wrong about your last move. That is the work; the rest is consolation. A good brief earns the reader’s second reading.

V2  ·  lh 1.75

You came in asking whether to leave the job. The job is the symptom. The decision underneath — and the only one you can act on this quarter — is whether to lower the cost of being wrong about your last move. That is the work; the rest is consolation. A good brief earns the reader’s second reading.

The V1 setting (1.78) reads slightly “floaty” — paragraphs land softer than they should. 1.75 is the publication standard for Source Serif at 17 px, and stays consistent with the Brand Brief.

III  ·  body-large · new19 px / 1.80

A new step for KEY Letter (Brief 15) and any long-form reading surface where 17 px feels tight. Used sparingly — not the default body.

.t-body-large   ·   19 / 34.2   ·   max-width 36em

You came in asking whether to leave the job. The job is the symptom. The decision underneath — and the only one you can act on this quarter — is whether to lower the cost of being wrong about your last move.

We say so plainly because that is the work; the rest is consolation. A good brief earns the reader’s second reading. A great one earns the reader’s pen — annotations in the margin, the kind of underlines that survive the move into a folder beside the bed.

你来的时候问的是要不要离职。离职是症状。
真正的问题，是你愿不愿意降低上一次判断错的成本 —— 这一句不是劝告，是这份简报存在的理由。

When to use: KEY Letter (Brief 15) body, /methodology long-form, the opening manifesto on any Brief PDF. Anywhere the reader stays for many minutes.

When not to use: defaults, cards, captions, nav, app surfaces. The default body stays 17 px.
